How to Play

Four Steps to Victory

Dead simple to learn. Devastatingly hard to master. Here's the short version.

01

Match Color or Number

Play a card that matches either the color or number of the top discard. Simple — until it isn't.

02

Use Action Cards

Skip opponents. Reverse the order. Force extra draws. Wild cards let you call any color — total power move.

03

Shout UNO!

One card left? Smash that UNO button FAST. Forget and get caught — draw 2. No mercy. No exceptions.

04

Go Out & Win

Play your last card. Glory is yours. Your opponents score the cards in their hands. Lowest total wins the round.

FAQ

Got Questions? We've Got Answers.

Is UNO free to play?
Absolutely — 100% free forever. Jump in from any browser with zero downloads. We offer cosmetic upgrades (card skins, avatars, animated backgrounds) but every game mode is free. No pay-to-win, ever.
How many players can join a single game?
2 to 10 players per room. Private rooms let you invite friends with a code, while Quick Match throws you into a live table in under 10 seconds. Tournament brackets support up to 128 players.
What rule sets are available?
Classic UNO rules ship by default. Custom mode lets you toggle: Draw stacking (+2/+4 chains), Jump-In (play out of turn), Seven-Zero (swap hands), No-Bluffing challenges, and Speed mode with 15-second turn timers.
Is there a mobile app?
iOS and Android apps are in open beta right now — download from the App Store or Google Play. Your account, progress, and cosmetics sync perfectly across all platforms via a single UNO account.
How do tournaments work?
Weekday daily cups (free entry, 32 players) and Weekend Grand Slam (prize pool, 128 players) run every week. You earn ranking points, and the weekly Top 10 get UNO Coins redeemable for cosmetics and real-money gift cards.
